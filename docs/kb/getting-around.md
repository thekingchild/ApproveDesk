# Getting around

The sidebar is the same on every signed-in screen. Use it to move between your work and, if you have access, administration.

## Public website

The site address opens **Login** by default. If you are already signed in, it opens **Dashboard** instead.

An administrator can turn on **Show public landing page** under **Settings → Branding**. When it is on, the site address shows the public marketing page: guests see **Log in** and, when self-registration is allowed, **Sign up**; signed-in people see **Dashboard**.

Branding turns the public page on or off but does not edit its content.

## Home

Dashboard content changes with your role.

- Approvers see work that needs a decision first.
- Requesters see drafts and returned requests first.
- Administrators see organization shortcuts and operational health, including **Administration → Operations**, **Administration → Backups**, and **Administration → Updates**.
- People with more than one role see approvals first, then their own requests.

## Sidebar

On a desktop or laptop, select the visible navigation control beside the company name to collapse the sidebar and give the page more horizontal space. The control remains visible in the compact sidebar; select it again to expand the menu. ApproveDesk remembers this choice in that browser. Menu icons retain their labels as tooltips in compact mode.

On a phone or narrow window, use the menu button in the header. Desktop collapse does not replace or change the mobile drawer.

## Search

Open search from the sidebar, or press Command-K / Control-K. Search looks at reference numbers, titles, requesters, status, and configured form fields you are allowed to see. Results only include requests your account can open.

## Notifications

The bell in the sidebar shows unread count. Open it to read items, mark them read, and follow the link into the request. Delivery of email and digest copies is controlled in your account notification settings.

## Times and time zones

**What it is.** Times on screens (comments, request history, the workflow timeline, notifications, delegation and out-of-office periods) are shown in your device's own time zone. ApproveDesk remembers it from your browser, so it stays right after you sign in again.

**How to use it.** Hover over a time to see the exact date, time, and time zone. Times you type into date-and-time fields are read in the same zone.

**What not to expect.** Printed approval reports, analytics exports, backup schedules, and approval-settings working hours use the installation time zone, which those screens name. Due dates are calendar dates and never move between days.

## Help

Help sits at the bottom of the sidebar, next to Notifications. It does not add another Administration item. On phones it is the life-ring icon in the header. The menu opens a short list of guides; **Help centre** lists every article you can read.

Administrators also see a life-ring on Administration screens. It opens the guide for that screen without changing the page layout. Request pages include a life-ring for collaboration (followers, comments, reminders, out of office).
