# Announcements

An **announcement** is an in-app message shown at the top of the signed-in **Dashboard**. Announcements are separate from approval notifications: they are not emailed or sent to Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, or Telegram.

Use announcements to read policy changes, planned maintenance, deadlines, and other organization notices while working in ApproveDesk.

## Display modes

- **Sticky** remains on the Dashboard until it is ended. You cannot dismiss it.
- **Acknowledge** remains until you select **Mark as read**.
- **Show once** appears on your first eligible Dashboard load and is then hidden. Opening another page does not consume it.

A role-targeted announcement appears when you hold at least one of its intended roles. If that role is removed before you see it, the message may no longer apply to you.

A long message starts as a compact excerpt. Select **Show more** to expand it and **Show less** to collapse it. Expanding or collapsing does not mark an Acknowledge message as read.

## What not to do

- Do not treat an announcement as an approval step or proof that work was completed.
- Do not copy passwords, access tokens, private keys, or unnecessary personal information from a notice.
- Do not assume a Dashboard announcement was also delivered by email or chat.
