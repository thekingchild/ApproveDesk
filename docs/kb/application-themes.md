# Application themes

An **application theme** is an accessible color palette used on signed-in ApproveDesk pages. It changes accents, links, focus indicators, and related application colors. It does not replace the logo, favicon, login background, or public website.

The **organization default theme** is used when you have not selected another available theme. Your palette choice is separate from Light, Dark, or System appearance mode.

## Choose your theme

1. Open **Settings → Appearance**.
2. Under **Light and dark mode**, choose Light, Dark, or System. System follows your device.
3. Under **Application theme**, choose **Organization default** or another available palette.
4. Select **Save application theme**.
5. Open or reload a signed-in page to apply the saved palette.

If a selected palette later becomes unavailable, ApproveDesk safely uses the current organization default. Your Light, Dark, or System choice remains independent.

Available palettes are designed to keep foreground and primary colours at least 4.5:1 contrast. Built-in palettes cannot be archived.

## What not to do

- Do not rely on color alone to communicate approval status or urgency.
- Do not expect an application theme to change Login or public-site branding.
- Do not use browser appearance mode as a replacement for an application palette; they control different parts of the display.
