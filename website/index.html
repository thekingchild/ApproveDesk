What teams use it for

  • Configurable forms

    Request types with a visual form builder. Requesters pick a form; they never pick a workflow.

  • Visual approval chains

    Sequential, parallel, quorum, conditional, and automatic steps. Published versions stay frozen.

  • Decisions from anywhere

    Email, Teams, Slack, WhatsApp, or Telegram. Signed one-click approve still checks the current step.

  • Immutable history

    Every action is appended. State is replayed, not rewritten. Evidence survives the person who left.

How it works

  1. 1

    Design the request

    Administrators publish a form and assign the workflow that new submissions will follow.

  2. 2

    Submit and route

    People fill the form, attach files, and the engine starts the published chain — not a chat thread.

  3. 3

    Decide and keep proof

    Approvers act in the workbench or from a signed link. The trail stays append-only.
